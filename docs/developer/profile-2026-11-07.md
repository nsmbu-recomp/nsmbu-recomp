## Why the save stops the picture

`crashrec::service` in `runtime/src/crash/crashrec.cpp` requests a save after 120 seconds.
Slots 101 and 102 are the automatic slots.
`kAutoBase` is 100.
`do_save` in `runtime/src/save/savestate.cpp` freezes the guest threads first.
It then copies heaps, files, audio, GX2 state, and guest RAM into one buffer.
It thaws the threads after the copy.
The swap gap matches the copy time, not the later disk write.
The disk write runs on a background thread.
`flips still pending after 300 ms` is the GX2 drain while the threads stay frozen.
That line is a result of the freeze.
