### What happens

When changing the aspect ratio—to 16:9, 16:10, 21:9, or 32:9—lines appear on the sides, obstructing the image. Furthermore, in the world selection menu, the image gets cut off; this happens regardless of the aspect ratio chosen, (As an example, here are the images: the first one is in 21:9, and the second one is in 16:9.) 

Also, in 21:9 aspect ratio, objects in the levels appear as if the game were set to 16:9 (As an example, look at the last two images: you can see that Mario has barely moved forward, yet the flowers appear suddenly—as if it were 16:9, even though it was set to 21:9.)

### Steps to reproduce

1: Start the game
2: Go to the world selection screen (the first visual error appears here)
3: Once on the world map, lines appear along the sides, obstructing the screen.
4: When loading a level in 21:9 and moving forward, you will notice that objects appear—suddenly popping into view—within the 16:9 aspect ratio area.

### Where you were

Basically, anywhere on the world map—I had reached the desert, but upon returning to the first world, the lines are still there.
Furthermore, the issue where elements appear gradually while playing in a 21:9 aspect ratio occurs even with enemies, and on any level.
