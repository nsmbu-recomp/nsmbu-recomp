# Wider or taller picture (experimental)

Aspect ratio is under **Settings → Display → Aspect ratio (experimental)**.

## Shapes

| Choice | Result |
| --- | --- |
| 16:9 (original) | The Wii U TV picture |
| Match window | Fills the window shape |
| 16:10, 21:9, 32:9 | Fixed wider shapes |

## Stretch or widen the view

**Stretch picture instead of widening the view** (default on) stretches the 16:9 TV picture to fill the chosen shape.
The GamePad picture keeps its own shape.

Turn stretch **off** to show more of the level in a wider or taller shape instead of stretching pixels.

`NSMBU_ASPECT` sets a ratio for one start, for example `21:9`.
